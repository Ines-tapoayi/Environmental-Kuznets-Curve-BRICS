# Environmental-Kuznets-Curve-Brics
## Overview
This project investigates the relationship between economic growth and environmental degradation in BRIC countries:

- Brazil
- Russia
- India
- China

The study evaluates the validity of the Environmental Kuznets Curve (EKC) hypothesis using both econometric and machine learning approaches.

## Objectives
- Test the existence of an inverted-U EKC relationship
- Measure carbon decoupling dynamics
- Estimate turning points in emissions trajectories
- Compare econometric and neural network approaches
- Analyze the pollution haven hypothesis

## Methodology

### Econometric Approach
- Fixed effects panel model
- Quadratic GDP specification
- Elasticity analysis
- Turning point estimation

### Machine Learning
- Neural network modeling
- Non-parametric estimation
- Non-linear relationship detection

## Key Findings

### EKC Validation
- Positive GDP coefficient
- Negative quadratic GDP coefficient
- Statistical validation of the inverted-U relationship

### Turning Point
Estimated turning point:
- ~83,264 USD GDP per capita

This level remains far above current BRIC income levels.

### Decoupling
- Relative decoupling observed
- No evidence of absolute decoupling

### Neural Networks
- Better capture country-specific trajectories
- Detect non-linear and heterogeneous dynamics
- Improve flexibility compared to rigid polynomial models

## Main Conclusion
Economic growth alone is insufficient to achieve climate objectives in BRIC countries.

Strong climate policies remain necessary:
- carbon taxation
- green investment
- technology transfer
- energy transition policies
